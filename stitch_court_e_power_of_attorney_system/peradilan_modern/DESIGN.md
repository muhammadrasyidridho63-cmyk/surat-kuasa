## Brand & Style
The design system reflects the statutory dignity, integrity, and ceremonial gravity of the Indonesian judicial system, recast through the lens of modern civic technology. It balances sovereign authority with radical clarity, eliminating bureaucratic friction for legal practitioners, registrars, and citizens navigating power of attorney (*Surat Kuasa Khusus* and *Surat Kuasa Insidentil*).

The aesthetic marries **Corporate / Modern GovTech** precision with subtle judicial heritage:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, transparent, incorruptible, and calm.
- **Visual Stance:** Crisp border structures reminiscent of high-end fintech dashboards, deliberate visual hierarchy, and luminous white surfaces contrasted against deep, grounded institutional greens and restrained warm gold accents.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires procedural certainty, trust in legal authenticity, and effortless navigation through multi-tier verification workflows.

## Colors
The color architecture pairs a sovereign forest green with a refined judicial gold, anchored by cool slate undertones for structured legibility.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#0D5236`):** The judicial anchor. Used for primary actions, header navigation, active legal states, and core branding elements. Interacts with `#0A3F29` on active/hover states.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#C8963E`):** The hallmark of judicial authority and certified stamps. Reserved for verification seals, key badges, highlight callouts, and secondary decorative badges. Interacts with `#B58432` on hover.
- **Neutral Surface & Text:**
  - Background Canvas: `#F8FAFC` (Soft Slate Gray)
  - Surface & Cards: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
  - Text Primary: `#111827` (Deep Slate Charcoal)
  - Text Secondary: `#64748B` (Muted Steel)
  - Border & Dividers: `#E2E8F0` (Neutral Gray)

### Functional & Legal Status Tiers
Status badges use deliberate surface-to-border pairings to establish immediate procedural comprehension without visual noise:
- **Disahkan (Approved / Certified):** Background `#DCFCE7`, Text `#166534`, Border `#86EFAC`.
- **Menunggu (Pending Review):** Background `#FEF9C3`, Text `#854D0E`, Border `#FDE047`.
- **Perlu Revisi (Needs Revision):** Background `#E0F2FE`, Text `#0369A1`, Border `#7DD3FC`.
- **Ditolak (Rejected):** Background `#FEE2E2`, Text `#991B1B`, Border `#FCA5A5`.

## Typography
The typographic system pairs the structural warmth and clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent headers with the analytical neutrality of **Inter** for dense legal forms, verification manifests, and data tables.

- **Headers & Ceremonial Titles:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans with tighter tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to project modern executive presence.
- **Legal Content & Form Data:** Set in Inter to maintain optimal optical legibility across complex document review fields, case numbers (*Nomor Perkara*), and multi-paragraph clause reviews.
- **Numbers & Case References:** Utilize tabular figure styling (`tnum`) in Inter for financial, stamp (*Meterai*), and registry identifiers.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a fluid 12-column grid constrained to a maximum content container of `1280px` on desktop, ensuring dense application dashboards remain focused and digestible.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) page margin. Sidebars for navigation and status feeds remain fixed or sticky, while form steps and document previews dynamically utilize the remaining columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters. Complex two-column forms reflow into unified vertical flows.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` (16px) margins and gutters. Legal review split-screens switch to sequential tabbed steps.
- **Component Rhythm:** Interior card paddings strictly observe `1.5rem` (`space-lg`), with inline form groups spaced at `1rem` (`space-md`). Tight element relationships (e.g., icons next to labels) use `0.5rem` (`space-sm`).

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation conveys meticulous order and document sanctity rather than dramatic 3D space. Depth is achieved via **low-contrast outlines layered over soft, ambient, tinted shadows**.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the `#F8FAFC` page backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Data Tables):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a `1px` border of `#E2E8F0` and an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements, Dropdowns, Popovers):** Elevated with a subtle judicial tint: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(13, 82, 54, 0.04), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05)`, bounded by `#E2E8F0`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Legal Document Viewers):** Higher elevation over an institutional scrim (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.4)`): `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)`.

## Shapes
The shape hierarchy reinforces clean precision while preserving contemporary softness:

- **Cards & Document Panels:** Defined with `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px) to frame legal records clearly without harsh boxiness.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selects):** Defined with `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px) for an ergonomic, structured touch target.
- **Badges, Tags, & Status Pills:** Configured with `rounded-full` (`9999px`) to create a clear visual distinction between functional actions (squared/rounded) and descriptive states (pills).
- **Checkboxes:** Standard `0.25rem` (4px) rounded corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0D5236`, Text `#FFFFFF`, font-weight `500`. Hover state shifts to `#0A3F29`. Border radius `8px` (`rounded-lg`). Padding `0.625rem 1.25rem`. Focus ring uses `2px` offset with `#0D5236` at 30% opacity.
- **Secondary / Accent:** Background `#C8963E`, Text `#FFFFFF`. Hover state shifts to `#B58432`. Used exclusively for critical endorsements, digital signatures (*Tanda Tangan Elektronik*), and certification submissions.
- **Outlined / Tertiary:** Background `#FFFFFF`, Border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, Text `#111827`. Hover state shifts to `#F8FAFC` background with border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#FFFFFF`, Border `1px solid #FCA5A5`, Text `#991B1B`. Hover state shifts to `#FEE2E2`.

### Status Badges & Chips
Badges use `rounded-full` geometry with `0.25rem 0.75rem` padding, typography `label-sm`, and uppercase tracking:
- **Disahkan:** Background `#DCFCE7`, Border `1px solid #86EFAC`, Text `#166534`. Accompanied by a leading checkmark icon.
- **Menunggu:** Background `#FEF9C3`, Border `1px solid #FDE047`, Text `#854D0E`. Accompanied by a clock icon.
- **Perlu Revisi:** Background `#E0F2FE`, Border `1px solid #7DD3FC`, Text `#0369A1`. Accompanied by an alert circle icon.
- **Ditolak:** Background `#FEE2E2`, Border `1px solid #FCA5A5`, Text `#991B1B`. Accompanied by an X icon.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Container:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `8px` radius, typography `body-md`.
- **States:** Hover border shifts to `#CBD5E1`. Focus transitions to `1px solid #0D5236` with a `3px` outer ring in `rgba(13, 82, 54, 0.12)`.
- **Helper & Error Texts:** Standard helper text is `#64748B`. Error state switches border to `#EF4444`, with messaging in `#991B1B` and an error icon.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, surface `#FFFFFF`. Checked state fills `#0D5236` with white checkmark. Radios maintain concentric circular ring.

### Cards & Legal Document Containers
- **Standard Card:** Background `#FFFFFF`, Border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, Radius `12px` (`rounded-xl`), Padding `1.5rem`.
- **Court Document Preview Pane:** Background `#FFFFFF`, bordered with an inner shadow and subtle `#0D5236` header accent line (`3px` top border) signifying authenticated judicial files.

### Specialized Judicial Components
- **Digital Stamp (*Meterai Elektronik*) Anchor:** Dedicated dashed-border placeholder (`#CBD5E1`) turning to solid `#C8963E` upon valid e-meterai affixation.
- **Verification Stepper:** Linear multi-step timeline tracking the lifecycle of the *Surat Kuasa* (Pengajuan &rarr; Verifikasi Berkas &rarr; Pembayaran PNBP &rarr; Legalisasi Panitera). Active nodes rendered in `#0D5236`, completed in `#C8963E`, and pending in `#E2E8F0`.
